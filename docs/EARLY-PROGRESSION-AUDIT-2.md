# Follow-up progression audit - 6 November 2026

Scope: the current source, pinned installed Create 6.0.10, MI 2.5.11, Iron Furnaces 4.3.2 and Flux Networks 8.0.0 recipes, early quests and the supplied single-Overworld design. This is a recipe/source audit with targeted native tests, not a complete fresh survival playthrough. Compatibility recipes for absent mods are excluded. Natural spawning, trading and wandering traders are possible fallbacks; a missing controlled machine route is not necessarily an absolute vanilla impossibility.

Already addressed: Millstone Coke -> Coke Dust; wooden/steel Cow leather; steel Blaze rods; Iron/Steel Flux Dust every 128 completed outputs. These are implemented, not recommendations below. The following rows are proposals only, ordered by implementation priority.

## Evidence and distinctions

- `MachineKind.java` currently supports oak, four crop programs, Zombie/Skeleton/Spider/Cow, plus steel Blaze. It has no Enderman, Slime, Magma Cube or trading program. `ContainedHarvesterMob.java` keeps the entity outside the world entity list; native burner capture cannot reach it.
- Installed `create:crafting/kinetics/empty_blaze_burner` requires netherrack and iron plates. Rod production is not equivalent to burner capture.
- Installed Create `splashing/crushed_raw_gold` supplies quartz, but raw gold processing is a crushing-wheel recipe. Precision-mechanism byproducts also require machines with brass/electron-tube prerequisites. There is currently no raw-gold Millstone bridge.
- Installed MI `materials/mixer/coke_to_raw_oil`, `materials/blast_furnace/raw_oil_to_oil`, and `materials/mixer/oil_to_rubber_sheet` run at 2 EU/t machine rating; the last consumes paper. The steam-age machines are the intended early route. Native MI `compat/create/craft/belt` and compressor variant already accept six rubber sheets.
- MI ships ordinary crafting recipes for the LV Steam Turbine, motor and analog circuit (`electric_age/.../*_asbl`), in addition to assembler recipes. An assembler-only circular dependency should not be inferred from the generated assembler copies.
- `DrillTier.java` has no silver, nickel, lead, bauxite, quartz, emerald, magnetite or Ancient Debris entries. MI's Steam Quarry needs steel and an invar rotary blade; it is not an early substitute and conflicts with the intended RevoExtraction role.
- Wheat has a starter-grass/bone-meal path. Current Zombie mode already produces carrots and potatoes. These are not listed as missing resources.
- `revostorage/.../RevoStorage.java` is an empty mod entrypoint. Its planned workstation is a missing feature, not a current recipe deadlock.

Proposed counts are initial balance values, not claims that those recipes already exist. Final acceptance should include a fresh island playthrough through the first powered greenhouse; the targeted bridge tests do not establish whole-pack completion.
