# Revo machine ingredients

The registry audit covers 166 Revo block IDs. All 156 blocks with an item form have an acquisition route, including the hammer interaction for Dust. The other ten are world fluid blocks. Following the Revo recipe inputs from the machine blocks and their programme items covered 264 recipe files and 301 distinct explicit item, fluid and tag references. This is a recipe and source audit. A fresh-island survival run through the complete endgame remains unverified.

| Machine family | Special ingredients or operating supplies | Where they come from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rain barrel and crucible | Clay, unfired vessels and early stone | Early crafting and firing recipes. Hammer dirt for Stone Pebbles. |
| Composter and silkworm cultivator | Compost and Silk Cocoons | Feed compostable items or leaves to the corresponding machine. These are machine outputs used in other recipes. |
| Greenhouses, tree growers and trading colony | Crop starters, saplings and catalyst items | Use the matching starter or crop route. Cotton grows from craftable seeds, either as a crop or in the greenhouse. |
| Mob harvesters | Mob-specific spawners and later spawner tiers | Craft the corresponding egg and surround it with iron bars. The Wither Skeleton egg uses Netherite, soul sand and bone, giving a route to the first skull. The egg and placed spawner are disabled for mob spawning. |
| Mechanical drills, gravel feeder and sluice | Create components, steel parts, water and sediment hatch | Existing crafts and layered assembly guides. Iron and steel drills produce Mineral and Gem Sludge for native fan washing. |
| Electric and Digital drills | MI hatches and hulls, retained Survey Cores | Standard MI components plus the Deep, Tungsten and Nuclear Survey Core crafts. The Electric Drill makes Crystalline Sludge for native MI centrifuging. Late survey modes supply platinum, monazite and Ancient Debris. |
| Salt Geode Chamber | Saline Geode and seawater | Craft the geode. Mix drill sediment into seawater, concentrate it in the chamber and centrifuge the brine for salt. |
| Wither Hatchery | Wither Egg, eligible skulls and substantial power | The usual Wither block arrangement drops a Wither Egg. The Steel Harvester's Wither Skeleton programme supplies skulls. The powered hatchery yields Nether Stars. |
| Revo furnaces | The original tier's Iron Furnaces block, matching casings and native-style hatches | Controllers use the matching native furnace with eight casings. Rainbow uses the Million Furnace and Rainbow Plating route. Fuel hatches use solid, liquid or gas fuel according to the installed hatch. |
| Vacuums | Earlier vacuum tiers and standard transport parts | Each upgrade uses the prior vacuum. The top tier also uses diamonds. |
| Power converters | Flux Networks parts and MI electronics | Recipes load with the required mods. Both are installed in the pack. |
| Chest Drive and Crafting Terminal | Chests and Create parts | Existing controller recipes. Load the drive with chest items and charge the terminal through its Create connection or by winding it. |
| Nexus machines, Core and Builder | Reusable moulds, plating fluids, two target types, fusion plasma, containment cells, Astral Essence and Phase Lattices | Every custom intermediate has a recipe. The Core needs six native MI Singularities. The Builder requires the completed Core as a retained ingredient for the Construction Program, then a charged 100 million EU buffer and the full bill of materials. |

The seven explicit Revo ingredients with no recipe JSON output have gameplay sources. The composter produces Compost, the silkworm cultivator produces Silk Cocoons, cotton is harvested, the three sludge grades come from drills, and Stone Pebbles come from hammering dirt. Native MI, Create, AE2, Flux Networks and Iron Furnaces supply the referenced modded intermediates through their installed recipe catalogues.
