# Modern Industrialization progression

Open **MI Guide: Circuits, Power and Advanced Machines** in FTB Quests for the
five circuit milestones and the production branches that feed Chapter 9. Use
EMI for the installed recipes and the MI Guidebook for multiblock layouts and the
reactor fuel grid.

| Power tier | Circuit | Main circuit inputs | Matching hull | Native cable tier rating |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| LV | Analog Circuit | Analog board, resistors, capacitors, copper wire and an inductor | Basic Machine Hull | 32 EU/t |
| MV | Electronic Circuit | 4 Analog Circuits, 2 diodes, 2 transistors, 1 Electronic Circuit Board | Advanced Machine Hull | 128 EU/t |
| HV | Digital Circuit | 4 Electronic Circuits, 2 NOT Gates, 1 OR Gate, 1 AND Gate, 1 Digital Circuit Board | Turbo Machine Hull | 1,024 EU/t |
| EV | Processing Unit | 4 Digital Circuits, 2 RAM, 1 MMU, 1 ALU, 1 Processing Unit Board | Highly Advanced Machine Hull | 8,192 EU/t |
| SV (Superconductor) | Quantum Circuit | 4 Processing Units, 2 Cooling Cells, 2 Qubits, 1 Quantum Circuit Board | Quantum Machine Hull | 128,000,000 EU/t |

One Quantum Circuit embeds 256 Analog Circuits through the lower-tier circuit
recipes alone. Keep each circuit tier in production for machines, hatches and
replacement parts. The cable tier rating is a native electrical rating. Check
the machine's recipe draw, machine upgrades, energy hatch limits and generation
capacity when sizing a line.

## From steam to the first Digital Circuit

| Milestone | What to set up |
| --- | --- |
| Steam into LV | Use the Steel Drill for lead and antimony, then mix battery alloy. Build a Basic Machine Hull with an Analog Circuit and Redstone Batteries. Supply an LV Steam Turbine with enough steam for the first 32 EU/t line. |
| Early aluminum and silicon | Use the Electric Blast Furnace for bauxite and for silicon dust from quartz and coke. The first aluminum opens the Electrolyzer. Start with ordinary silicon plates for diodes and transistors. |
| Electronic Circuits and MV chemistry | Produce Electronic Circuit Boards and Silicon Batteries. Build the Advanced Hull and MV power. Set up sulfuric acid, oil refining and polyethylene. |
| Digital Circuits and HV refining | Finish hot stainless steel and Kanthal in the Vacuum Freezer. Use Kanthal coils for higher-draw EBF recipes. Feed polyethylene, stainless plates, Silicon Batteries and Electronic Circuit Boards into the Digital Circuit Board recipe. Prepare logic gates and Sodium Batteries for the Turbo Hull. |

Chapters 3–8 detail the machines and the order for commissioning them.

## Processing Units, nuclear power and Quantum Circuits

| Production line | What it supplies |
| --- | --- |
| Titanium, tungsten and survey cores | Electrolyze bauxite for tiny titanium dust. Pack it, smelt it in the EBF and finish hot ingots in the Vacuum Freezer. Use titanium for the Tungsten Survey Core. Tungsten and a Nether Star feed the Nuclear Survey Core. Retain the respective core in the Electric or Digital Drill. |
| Platinum and iridium | Drill raw platinum with the Nuclear Survey Core. Leach it with sulfuric acid in the Chemical Reactor. Centrifuging has a 5% chance to yield tiny iridium dust per 1,000 mB. Electrolyze the purified solution for tiny platinum dust and recover most of the acid. |
| Monazite and superconductors | The same survey core opens the Monazite program. Centrifuge nine dust into three each of cadmium, yttrium and neodymium. Save cadmium for batteries and control rods. Mix yttrium and neodymium with annealed copper and iridium for superconductor alloy, then blast-furnace, cool and wiremill it. |
| Air separation | A reusable Air Intake in the Vacuum Freezer produces liquid air. The Centrifuge splits it into nitrogen, oxygen and argon. Supply nitrogen to the caster and argon to silicon and Cryofluid. |
| Shale oil to helium | Run the native Oil Drilling Rig with a Stainless Steel Drill. Every 1,000 mB shale oil can yield 50 mB helium via a Distillery or Distillation Tower. Centrifuge helium for Helium-3. It takes 10,000 mB helium to make the 50 mB required by one Quantum Circuit Board. |
| Processing Unit Board and memory | Make PVC from acetylene and hydrochloric acid via Vinyl Chloride. Each board consumes 1,500 mB PVC, 4 platinum plates, 2 Cadmium Batteries, 8 annealed copper cables and a Digital Circuit Board. Grow Monocrystalline Silicon with argon and tiny iridium in the EBF. Cut it with lubricant into wafers for RAM and MMUs. Make ALUs from the diamond-and-logic-gate recipe. |
| Highly Advanced Hull and Nuclear Reactor | The first native Nuclear Reactor requires Processing Units and a Highly Advanced Hull. Drill uranium with the Nuclear Survey Core, make fuel rods, and commission the cooled reactor using the MI Guidebook. Centrifuge depleted rods for plutonium and recovered uranium. |
| Cryofluid, isotopes and Quantum Circuit Board | Make Cryofluid in the Vacuum Freezer from argon and helium. Feed it to Cooling Cells and Qubits. Centrifuge 1,000 mB hydrogen for 20 mB deuterium and 1 mB tritium. Qubits consume tritium. Each Quantum Circuit Board needs a Processing Unit Board, 12 superconductor cables, 2 Plutonium Batteries, 6 iridium plates and 50 mB Helium-3. |
| Quantum Hull and Fusion | Make the Quantum Hull with a Quantum Circuit, Quantum Casing, Plutonium Batteries and superconductor cable. Use Quantum hardware to commission Fusion, and turn deuterium into Helium Plasma and tritium. Keep helium gas and Helium Plasma in separate lines. |
| Implosion and UU Matter | Send an Ultradense Metal Ball and 64 native MI Nukes into the Implosion Compressor per Singularity. A native Electrolyzer uses a retained Singularity to make UU Matter. Keep enough Singularities for all six Nexus Ignition Cells. |

## Chapter 9 and the Nexus Core

| Order | Machine or milestone | What to feed and where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Pressure Casting Plant (9 × 9 × 5) | Mix the Nexus Alloy blend, blast-furnace it with UU Matter, then cool the ingots. Cast the alloy with moulds, nitrogen and Cryofluid. Reuse the moulds and finish the castings in the MI Compressor. Structural frames feed the next controller recipes. |
| 2 | Electroplating Line (9 × 9 × 5) | Assemble cast carriers with Create Precision Mechanisms and sludge-derived diamonds and Certus Quartz. Make the cleaning bath from the salt line, prepare the copper electrolyte and run clean, deposit and rinse. Turn substrates into contacts for the Collider and Core, and reserve more rinsed substrates for lattices. |
| 3 | Particle Collider (21 × 21 × 5) | Compress tungsten for one target, polarize iridium with neodymium for the other. Supply reusable containment cells, Helium Plasma from Fusion, Cryofluid and Astral Essence. Plan for 262,144 EU/t per active Collider. Centrifuge the output to recover exotic matter and return the cells. |
| 4 | Phase Lattices and the Nexus Core | Assemble exotic matter with rinsed substrates into lattices. Feed the Core from cast housing, layered contacts, Quantum Circuits, AE2 Engineering Processors and six Ignition Cells. Astral Essence ties the Wither Hatchery, mob harvester drops, crops, trees, silk and rubber into the chain. |
| 5 | Builder and Nexus Stabilized | Certify the program with the completed Core, then deposit the retained Core and the rest of the bill in the Builder. Use Box to check clearance. Charge 100,000,000 EU for construction and feed the formed portal 1,024 EU/t to earn Nexus Stabilized. |

The alloy bill uses **763,000 mB of UU Matter** for 763 batches. The stock
Electrolyzer recipe outputs **1 mB per 2,500 base ticks**. Plan UU Matter
production early and measure your actual output with upgrades and parallel
machines before committing to a build schedule.

bill, reusable components and waste-recovery loops.
